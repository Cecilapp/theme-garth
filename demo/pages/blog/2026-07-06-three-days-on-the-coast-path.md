title: Three days on the coast path
tags: [Walks]
---
Sixty kilometres, two campsites, one very generous café owner, and more wind than I thought possible in July.
<!-- break -->
I had wanted to walk this stretch of coast for years. The plan was simple: start at the harbour, follow the cliffs north, take the bus back.

## Day one

The first day was all climbs and descents, each cove separated from the next by a steep headland. I stopped counting the steps after the fourth staircase.

## Day two

Rain in the morning, then a long, flat section along the dunes. A café owner in a tiny village refused to let me pay for my soup because, she said, "nobody walks here in the rain unless they really mean it."

## Day three

The wind. It pushed me north for the last twenty kilometres, which was lucky, since that was the direction I was going.

I came back with sore knees, a sunburnt neck, and a notebook full of small drawings of rocks.
