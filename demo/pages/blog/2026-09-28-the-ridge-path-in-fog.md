---
title: The ridge path in fog
tags: [Walks]
---
I left before sunrise, hoping to see the valley from the ridge. Instead, the fog came up to meet me, and for three hours the world was twenty metres wide.
<!-- break -->
Walking in fog changes the scale of everything. You stop looking for views and start looking at what is near: the beads of water on a spider web, the bark of a single beech, the sound of a sheep you never see.

The ridge path is about eleven kilometres from the church to the old quarry. It is well marked, which is a good thing on a morning like this one. I counted the waymarks out of habit, and was glad of every one.

## What I carried

- A thermos of coffee, brewed a bit stronger than usual
- The notebook, a pencil, and a spare pencil
- A wool layer I did not need until the descent

## Lesson of the day

> Fog is not the absence of a view. It is a different view.

By eleven, the sun burned through above the quarry. I sat on a cold stone and watched the valley appear, field by field, like a photograph in a developing tray.
